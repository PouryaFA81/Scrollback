# Adding folders

[← Guide index](README.md)

Scrollback shows the videos, pictures and songs from **folders you choose**. It never looks at
anything else on its own.

## Add a folder

1. Open **Settings** with the gear at the top of the screen.
2. Under **Library**, tap **Folders**.
3. Tap **Add folder**.
4. Pick a folder in Android's folder picker and allow access.

You can add as many folders as you like. **Subfolders are included** automatically. Common places
for saved clips are `Movies/` or `Download/`.

You can also add a folder from the [file manager](file-manager.md): long-press the folder, then tap
**Add source**. Nextcloud folders can be added too; see [Nextcloud](nextcloud.md).

## What gets indexed

Scrollback builds a private index on your phone with each file's name, folder, size, dates and, for
videos, the length and resolution. Each folder in the **Folders** list shows how many videos and
pictures it holds and when it was last scanned. Audio files in your folders show up in the
[Music](music-servers.md) tab.

## Keep it up to date

Scrollback rescans your folders every time the app starts. To rescan right away, open **Settings**
and tap **Rescan now** under **Library**. It picks up new, changed and removed files.

## Remove a folder

1. Open **Settings → Folders**.
2. Tap **Remove folder** next to the folder.
3. Confirm with **Remove**.

Scrollback forgets the folder and its likes, notes and playlist entries. **Your files are not
deleted.**
