# The feeds

[← Guide index](README.md)

The **Feed** tab plays your videos full screen, one at a time. Swipe up for the next video and down
for the previous one.

## Random and Saved

At the top of the Feed tab there is a **Random | Saved** switch.

- **Random** shuffles all your videos. Tap the **shuffle** button next to the switch to reshuffle.
- **Saved** shows your videos by the date of the file, which is usually when you saved it. Tap the
  **arrows** button next to the switch to flip between **newest first** and **oldest first**.

### Watch one folder in order

In **Saved**, a folder chip (**All folders ▾**) sits under the top bar. Tap it to pick **All
folders**, one of your folders, or one of its subfolders. The feed then plays only that folder, in
saved order, newest or oldest first. Scrollback remembers your choice.

### The feed keeps your place

Random and Saved each remember where you were: the video you were on and how far into it you got.
Switching between Random and Saved, opening another tab, locking your phone, or Android closing
Scrollback in the background doesn't send you back to the top. Random also keeps its shuffled order
until you tap **shuffle**. Saved remembers a place for each folder and for newest or oldest first.

## Controls on a video

| Action | How |
|---|---|
| Pause or play | Tap the video. |
| Watch at 2× | Press and hold the video. A **2×** pill shows at the top while you hold, and the phone gives a short buzz. Let go to go back to your speed. |
| Like | Double-tap the video, or tap **Like** on the right. |
| Seek | Drag the thin progress line at the bottom. |
| Save to a playlist | Tap **Save**. See [Likes and playlists](likes-and-playlists.md). |
| Add a note or tags | Tap **Notes**. See [Notes, tags and search](notes-and-tags.md). |
| Share | Tap **Share** to open Android's share sheet. |
| Details | Tap **Info**. |

Below the buttons you can see the file name, its folder, how long ago it was saved, and any note or
tags you added.

### Speed, looping and auto scroll

The buttons in the top-right corner of the feed control playback:

- **Speed chip** (for example **1×**): tap it and choose 0.5×, 0.75×, 1×, 1.25×, 1.5× or 2×. The
  speed is remembered and applies to every feed.
- **Playback button** (the loop icon): tap it to open a small menu.
  - **Auto scroll**: when it's on, the feed moves to the next item by itself. A video moves on when
    it ends, even if looping is on. A picture moves on after **3, 5 or 10 seconds** (you choose).
    The button turns blue while auto scroll is on.
  - **When a video ends**: **Loop it** or **Play the next one**. This applies when auto scroll is
    off.

Auto scroll waits while you're touching the screen, holding for 2× or have a sheet (like Notes or
Info) open, and carries on when you let go or close it. It's off by default. You can also turn it
on, and pick the picture time, in **Settings → Playback**.

### Keep the screen on

While a video plays, your phone's screen stays on, so it doesn't go dark in the middle of a long
video. When you pause, the screen can turn off again as usual. This works in the feed,
[Scrollback Player](scrollback-player.md) and [Watch](watch.md). Turn it off in **Settings →
Playback → Keep screen on while playing**.

## The Info sheet

Tap **Info** to see the **Details** of the current item: name, folder, type, size, duration,
resolution, when it was modified and indexed, and how often you have watched it. From here you can
also:

- **Hide** the item (or **Unhide** it). This needs a PIN; see
  [PIN lock and hidden items](pin-and-hidden-items.md).
- **Play in full player**, for videos. It opens the video in [Scrollback Player](scrollback-player.md)
  with gestures, subtitles, audio tracks and picture-in-picture, with the rest of the folder as the
  playlist.
- **Download** a copy, for items streamed from [Nextcloud](nextcloud.md).
- **Delete file**. Scrollback asks **Delete this file?** before anything happens.

> **Careful:** **Delete file** permanently deletes the file from its folder. For a Nextcloud item,
> it deletes the file **on the server**.

If the feed can't play a video's format, Scrollback opens it in
[Scrollback Player](scrollback-player.md) automatically.

## Resume where you left off

Scrollback remembers how far you got in each video. When you come back to it, playback continues
from that point. The Feed tab also remembers which video you were on; see
[The feed keeps your place](#the-feed-keeps-your-place). Every video you watch is listed under **Library → History**.

## Pictures

The **Pictures** tab shows every picture from your folders in a grid. Use the **Newest / Oldest**
button at the top to change the order. Tap a picture to open it full screen, then swipe up or down
to move between pictures. The same Like, Save, Notes, Share and Info buttons work on pictures too.

## Other feeds

Liked items, playlists, your history, search results and hidden items all open in the same
full-screen feed, with a **Back** button in the top-left corner.
