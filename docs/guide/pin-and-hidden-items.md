# PIN lock and hidden items

[← Guide index](README.md)

## Set a PIN

1. Open **Settings** with the gear at the top.
2. Under **Privacy**, tap **Set a PIN**.
3. Enter a PIN of **4 to 8 digits**, enter it again under **Confirm PIN**, and tap **Save**.

Scrollback stores only a salted hash of your PIN, never the PIN itself.

Once a PIN is set, the **Privacy** section offers more options:

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Change PIN** | Set a new PIN. |
| **Remove PIN** | Turns the lock off. Hidden items become visible everywhere again. |
| **Require PIN to open** | Asks for the PIN (**Enter PIN**) when you open the app, and again after the app has been in the background for **more than 30 seconds**. |
| **Show hidden items** | Reveals hidden items after you enter your PIN, until the app locks again. |

> **Don't forget your PIN.** There is no PIN recovery in the app.

## Hide an item

Hidden items need a PIN, so set one first.

1. In a feed, tap **Info** on the item.
2. Tap **Hide**.

The item disappears from the feeds, Pictures, Liked, playlists and search. To bring it back, reveal
hidden items (see below), open the item and tap **Unhide** in its Info sheet.

## See hidden items

There are three ways to reveal hidden items with your PIN:

- **Library → Hidden items** (shown only when a PIN is set). When locked it says **Locked — enter
  your PIN to view**; tap it and enter your PIN to open your hidden items as a feed.
- The **lock** button at the top of the **Library** tab.
- **Settings → Privacy → Show hidden items**.

Hidden items stay visible until you tap the lock button again, or until Scrollback has been in the
background for more than 30 seconds.

## Block screenshots

**Settings → Privacy → Block screenshots** stops screenshots and screen recordings of Scrollback, and
hides its content in the recent-apps view. This works with or without a PIN.
