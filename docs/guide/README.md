# Scrollback user guide

Welcome! This guide walks you through everything Scrollback can do today, screen by screen. It
describes **v0.14.0**.

> Scrollback is young, and many features are new and still in early testing, especially the server
> connections, Watch, Nearby share and SMB. If something does not work the way this guide says, please
> [open a bug report](https://github.com/PouryaFA81/Scrollback/issues/new/choose).

## Find your way around

Scrollback has up to five tabs at the bottom of the screen:

| Tab | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Feed** | Your videos, full screen. Switch between **Random** and **Saved** at the top. |
| **Pictures** | Your pictures in a grid, with a full-screen viewer. |
| **Music** | Songs, artists, albums, folders and playlists. You can hide this tab. |
| **Files** | A built-in file manager, with **Tools** for notes, archives, Office documents, network storage, Nearby share and file conversion. |
| **Library** | **Watch** (Jellyfin movies and shows), Liked, Playlists, History, Search, Duplicates and Hidden items. |

**Settings** open from the **gear** button at the top of the screen. Videos you open from Files or
from other apps play in [Scrollback Player](scrollback-player.md).
