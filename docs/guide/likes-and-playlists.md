# Likes and playlists

[← Guide index](README.md)

## Likes

- **Double-tap** a video or picture to like it, or tap **Like** on the right side of the feed.
- Tap **Like** again to unlike.
- Everything you like is collected in **Library → Liked**. Tap an item there to watch your likes as
  a feed.

Songs can be liked too, from the Music tab or the full player. If you use a music server, likes stay
in sync with stars (Subsonic) or favourites (Jellyfin). See
[Music and music servers](music-servers.md).

## Playlists

### Save an item to a playlist

1. In any feed, tap **Save** (the bookmark).
2. The **Save to playlist** sheet opens.
   - Tap a playlist to add the item. Tap it again to take the item out. A check mark shows which
     playlists already contain it.
   - To make a new playlist, type a name in **New playlist** and tap **+** (Create playlist). The
     item is added to it straight away.

The bookmark is filled in when the item is in at least one playlist.

### Manage playlists

Open **Library** and tap **Playlists**.

- Tap **New playlist** to create an empty one.
- Tap a playlist to open it. There you can:
  - **Play** it as a feed, or tap any item to start from there.
  - **Move up** / **Move down** to reorder items.
  - **Remove** an item from the playlist.
  - **Rename** the playlist.
  - **Delete** the playlist. The videos themselves stay on your device.

Playlists are shared with the Music tab, so you can add songs to them too.

## History

**Library → History** lists the videos you have watched, most recent first. Videos resume where you
left off. Tap **Clear history** to empty the list; this also resets the resume positions.
