# Getting started

[← Guide index](README.md)

## What you need

- An Android phone or tablet running **Android 13 or newer**.
- A folder with videos or pictures you want to watch, for example the folder your downloaded
  clips are saved to.

## 1. Install Scrollback

Signed release APKs are not available yet (**APK downloads on the Releases page: coming soon**).
For now, install a debug build as described in the
[README's install section](../../README.md#install).

## 2. Add your first folder

When you open Scrollback for the first time, the feed is empty and says **Nothing here yet**.

1. Tap **Add folders**.
2. On the **Folders** screen, tap **Add folder**.
3. Android's folder picker opens. Go to the folder with your saved videos and pictures and allow
   access.

Scrollback scans the folder, including all of its subfolders, and your videos appear in the feed.
See [Adding folders](adding-folders.md) for more.

## 3. Start watching

- Swipe up and down on the **Feed** tab to move between videos.
- Tap **Random** or **Saved** at the top to switch between a shuffled feed and the order you saved
  your videos in.
- Double-tap a video to like it.

Read [The feeds](feeds.md) for all the controls.

## 4. Look around

- **Pictures** shows the pictures from your folders.
- **Library** keeps your liked videos, playlists, watch history and search.
- **Files** is a file manager for your phone's storage.
- **Music** plays the songs in your folders and, if you like, from your own music server.
- The **gear** at the top opens **Settings**.

## Everything works offline

You don't need an account or an internet connection. Servers such as Nextcloud, Subsonic-compatible
servers and Jellyfin are optional extras; see [Nextcloud](nextcloud.md) and
[Music and music servers](music-servers.md).
