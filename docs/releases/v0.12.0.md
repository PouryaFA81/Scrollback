# Scrollback v0.12.0: Watch

Watch movies and shows from your own Jellyfin server, right inside Scrollback.

## What's new

- **Watch**, at the top of the **Library** tab. Sign in once under **Settings → Servers**. A
  Jellyfin account you already added for music works here too. With more than one Jellyfin account,
  switch with the ⇄ button.
- **Home** shows Continue watching, Next up, Recently added and Favorites. **Movies**, **Shows** and
  **Collections** are poster grids; swipe left or right to move between them. **Search** finds
  movies, shows, episodes and collections.
- **Details pages** show the backdrop, year, runtime, rating, genres, overview and cast. Shows list
  their seasons and episodes with progress, plus a button for the next episode.
- **Mark played / unplayed** and **Favorite** are saved on your server.
- **Smart playback.** Scrollback plays the original file when your phone can handle it, and asks
  Jellyfin to convert it on the fly when it can't (or when the phone fails to play it). The top bar
  shows **Direct play** or **Transcoding**.
- **Tracks.** Choose audio and subtitle tracks, including external subtitle files. Picture-based
  subtitles are drawn in by the server when needed.
- **Resume anywhere.** Pick up where you left off. Your progress syncs back to Jellyfin, so other
  Jellyfin apps start at the same spot.
- Your sign-in stays encrypted on your phone and is never put in links.

## Good to know

- Watch was tested with automated tests and a simulated Jellyfin server, not yet with a real
  Jellyfin server. Please report anything that doesn't work.
- A full movie player, with gestures, subtitle styling and picture-in-picture, followed in v0.13.0.

## Install

Scrollback needs **Android 13 or newer**. Download the APK for your phone from the
[Releases page](https://github.com/PouryaFA81/Scrollback/releases), open it, and allow
**Install unknown apps** when Android asks. If you already have the release build, you can also
update from **Settings → Updates → Check for update**. See the
[README](https://github.com/PouryaFA81/Scrollback#download) for which APK to pick and how to install
with adb.
