# Scrollback v0.4.0: music

Scrollback now plays music too, from your phone and from your own servers.

## What's new

- **Music tab** with Songs, Artists, Albums, Folders and Playlists, plus Liked songs, Recently
  played and search.
- **Background playback** with a media notification and lock-screen controls.
- **Mini player and full player** with artwork, a seek bar, shuffle, repeat (all or one), like and
  add to playlist.
- **Queue**: play next, add to queue, reorder and remove songs.
- **Your music, wherever it is:**
  - songs in your local folders, with their tags and embedded cover art
  - Nextcloud folders you've added
  - **Subsonic / OpenSubsonic** servers such as Navidrome, Nextcloud Music, Airsonic and Gonic
    (stars on the server stay in sync with your likes)
  - **Jellyfin** music libraries (favourites stay in sync with your likes; Scrollback keeps only an
    access token, never your password)
- Likes and playlists work for songs just like they do for videos.
- **Show Music** in Settings hides the Music tab if you don't need it.
- Starting a video pauses your music.

## Good to know

- Music and the music-server connections are brand new and still in early testing. Please report
  anything odd.
- Jellyfin is used for music only for now. Jellyfin videos in the feeds are coming soon.

## Install

Scrollback needs **Android 13 or newer**. There is no signed APK for this release yet (APK downloads on the Releases page: coming soon). To try it, build a debug APK with `./scripts/build-apk.sh` and install it with `adb install -r dist/scrollback-debug.apk`. See the [README](https://github.com/PouryaFA81/Scrollback#install) for details.
