# Scrollback v0.3.0: Nextcloud

Connect your own Nextcloud to stream more videos and pictures and keep your library in sync. It's
completely optional: Scrollback stays fully usable offline and without an account.

## What's new

- **Sign in with your Nextcloud's own login page** (two-factor login and single sign-on work) from
  **Settings → Servers**. Scrollback receives an app password, stored encrypted on your phone. You
  can revoke it any time in Nextcloud under Settings → Security.
- **Browse your Nextcloud** and add any folder as a Scrollback source. Its videos and pictures
  **stream** straight into your feeds, no download needed.
- **Download** any Nextcloud item to **Download/Scrollback** to play and share it offline.
- **Library sync**: likes, hidden items, notes, tags, history and playlists stay in sync between
  your devices through a single file in your Nextcloud. The most recent change wins.

## Permissions

This version adds the **internet** and **network state** permissions. They are used only for the
servers you add yourself.

## Good to know

- Nextcloud support is new and still in early testing. Please report anything that doesn't work.

## Install

Scrollback needs **Android 13 or newer**. No APK was published for this version. Install the [latest release](https://github.com/PouryaFA81/Scrollback/releases/latest) instead; see the [README](https://github.com/PouryaFA81/Scrollback#download) for which APK to pick.
