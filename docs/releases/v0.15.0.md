# Scrollback v0.15.0: auto scroll, hold for 2×, and a feed that keeps your place

## What's new

- **Auto scroll.** The feed can move on by itself: a video moves to the next item when it ends
  (even if looping is on), and a picture after **3, 5 or 10 seconds**. It waits while you touch the
  screen, hold for 2× or have a sheet open. Turn it on from the feed's **playback button** (top
  right, the loop icon) or **Settings → Playback**. It's off by default.
- **Press and hold for 2×.** Hold anywhere on a playing video in the feed, Scrollback Player or
  Watch to watch at 2×. A small **2×** pill shows while you hold, and letting go returns to your
  speed.
- **Keep screen on while playing.** Long videos no longer go dark halfway through. The screen stays
  on while a video plays in the feed, Scrollback Player and Watch, and can sleep again when you
  pause. On by default; you can turn it off in **Settings → Playback**.
- **Watch one folder in Saved order.** **Feed → Saved** has a folder chip under the top bar. Pick
  **All folders**, one folder, or a subfolder, and the feed plays just that folder, newest or
  oldest first. Your choice is remembered.
- **The feed keeps your place.** Random and Saved each remember the video you were on and how far
  in you were. Switching between them, opening another tab, locking your phone, or Android closing
  the app in the background no longer sends you back to the top. Random keeps its shuffled order
  until you tap shuffle.
- **Home servers on Android 17.** Adding or testing a server, connecting to SFTP or SMB, or using
  your own VERT server on your home network now asks for Android's **local network** permission,
  with a short explanation first. If you say no, Scrollback tells you how to turn it on instead of
  failing quietly. Servers you already added are checked once when you open the app. See
  [Local network access](https://github.com/PouryaFA81/Scrollback/blob/main/docs/guide/server-connections.md#local-network-access-android-17).
- **Real HW / SW label in Scrollback Player.** The top bar now shows the decoder that's actually in
  use. Formats your phone has no hardware decoder for (such as MPEG-2, or AV1 on most phones) show
  **SW**.
- **Security contact.** A new [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/PouryaFA81/Scrollback/blob/main/SECURITY.md)
  explains how to report a security problem privately, at admin@homelabgate.lol.
- **Screenshots.** The README and the user guide now have real screenshots of this version.

## Fixed

- The feed jumped back to the first video after locking and unlocking the phone, or after
  switching between Random and Saved.
- On Android 17, SFTP, SMB and home servers might not connect until Nearby share had been opened
  once.

## Good to know

- The feed's loop button now opens a small menu with **Auto scroll**, the picture time, and
  **Loop it** or **Play the next one** for when a video ends.
- **Downloads:** three signed APKs plus a debug build and `SHA256SUMS`. arm64-v8a (most phones from
  the last few years) is about 34 MB, armeabi-v7a (older 32-bit phones) about 31 MB, and
  universal (any phone) about 54 MB.

## Install

Scrollback needs **Android 13 or newer**. Download the APK for your phone from the
[Releases page](https://github.com/PouryaFA81/Scrollback/releases), open it, and allow
**Install unknown apps** when Android asks. If you already have the release build, you can also
update from **Settings → Updates → Check for update**. See the
[README](https://github.com/PouryaFA81/Scrollback#download) for which APK to pick and how to install
with adb.
