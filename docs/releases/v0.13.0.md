# Scrollback v0.13.0: Scrollback Player

Scrollback now has its own full movie player, built on libVLC. It plays almost any video: HEVC, VP9,
AV1, MPEG-2, VC-1, AVI, WMV, MKV and more, with ASS/SSA subtitles in their original styling.

## Where you'll find it

- **Open with → Scrollback Player** for any video file on your phone.
- Tap a video in **Files**: the rest of the folder becomes the playlist. Or select videos and tap
  **Play** (use **Open with** for another app).
- **Play in full player** in a feed video's Info sheet.
- If the feed can't play a video's format, it opens in Scrollback Player automatically.

## What it can do

- Swipe for brightness (left side), volume (right side) and seeking (sideways). Double-tap the sides
  to skip 10 seconds. Pinch to zoom.
- Aspect modes (Fit, Stretch, Crop, 16:9, 4:3, Original), rotation lock and frame capture (saved to
  `Pictures/Scrollback`).
- Choose audio and subtitle tracks, load a subtitle file, and adjust audio and subtitle delay.
- Subtitle size, colour, and a background box.
- Speed from 0.25× to 4×, A-B repeat, previous and next video.
- Picks up where you left off.
- Hardware or software decoding switch.
- Screen lock, picture-in-picture, and background audio with a notification.
- Works with local files, Nextcloud, and SFTP/SMB servers.

## Good to know

- **Bigger download.** The player adds about 20–24 MiB: arm64-v8a is now about 34 MiB, armeabi-v7a
  about 30 MiB and universal about 53 MiB. Most phones from the last several years need
  **arm64-v8a**.
- Scrollback Player isn't available on x86 devices, such as most emulators.
- Friendlier error messages for Jellyfin.
- **License note:** libVLC is LGPL-2.1-or-later and is included unmodified as separate shared
  libraries.

## Install

Scrollback needs **Android 13 or newer**. Download the APK for your phone from the
[Releases page](https://github.com/PouryaFA81/Scrollback/releases), open it, and allow
**Install unknown apps** when Android asks. If you already have the release build, you can also
update from **Settings → Updates → Check for update**. See the
[README](https://github.com/PouryaFA81/Scrollback#download) for which APK to pick and how to install
with adb.
